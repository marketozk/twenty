# LOG-211: Independent Design Review

## Verdict
`pass` (after resolving all 4 blockers, 3 majors, and 3 minors in `design.md` and `test-research.md`)

## Findings and resolutions

| # | Severity | Finding | Resolution in revised design |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | blocker | `withCrmAuditIdentity` rejects `workspaceMember` principals (`MembershipCleanupIdentity` only allows `coreUser`/`system`/`application`/`apiKey`/`agent`) and has no `impersonatorUserWorkspaceId` field | Eliminated `withCrmAuditIdentity` tunneling completely. Added `captureNativeCrmAuditBindingFromNavigationIdentity` producing a `WeakSet`-verified `NativeCrmAuditBinding` passed via `WorkspaceTransactionOptions.nativeAuditBinding`, with `coreUser` + `workspaceMember` impersonation support in `withNativeCrmAuditTransaction` and `recordPostRollbackNativeCrmAuditAttempt`. |
| B2 | blocker | Masking an active `withCrmAuditRequest` with `withCrmAuditIdentity` bypassed impersonation+agent and cross-user conflict guards in `captureNativeCrmAuditBinding` | ALS is never masked. `captureNativeCrmAuditBindingFromNavigationIdentity` reads `captureCurrentCrmAuditOperation(workspaceId)` directly and validates every explicit identity against it. |
| B3 | blocker | `RequestLikeAuditIdentity` mapping dropped `user`, `application`, and `impersonationContext`, breaking real HTTP GraphQL/REST `PageLayoutService.destroy` calls under `withCrmAuditRequest` | `captureNativeCrmAuditBindingFromNavigationIdentity` preserves `user`, `workspaceMemberId`, `application`, `viaApplication`, `apiKey`, `agentId`, `impersonatorUserWorkspaceId`, and `impersonationContext.impersonatorUserWorkspaceId`. |
| B4 | blocker | Importing `buildSystemAuthContext`, `WorkspaceAuthContext`, or `twenty-shared/utils` in new places adds Enterprise dependency edges failing `check_enterprise_dependencies.py --verify-baseline` | All new imports stay strictly within `src/engine/core-modules/crm-audit/*` and `is-transaction-outcome-unknown.ts` (`enterprise_reachable: false`). No `twenty-shared/utils` import added to `create-complete-dashboard.tool.ts`. |
| M1 | major | `PageLayoutService.destroy` committed `core.pageLayout` deletion before validating native audit identity for `destroyAssociatedDashboards`, and dropped `authUserWorkspaceId` | `PageLayoutService.destroy` now validates and captures `dashboardAuditBinding` (including `authUserWorkspaceId` via `pendingAuthUserWorkspaceId`) BEFORE `validateBuildAndRunWorkspaceMigration`. |
| M2 | major | Synthetic `user` `authContext` in `createCreateCompleteDashboardTool` broke `workspaceMember`-bound RLS and `userWorkspaceRoleMap`; late identity validation created/destroyed metadata unnecessarily | `createCreateCompleteDashboardTool` keeps `buildSystemAuthContext(context.workspaceId)` for `executeInWorkspaceContext`, passes `nativeAuditBinding` to `runInWorkspaceTransaction`, and captures/validates `nativeAuditBinding` at the start of `execute(...)` BEFORE `pageLayoutService.create`. |
| M3 | major | `DashboardDuplicationService`: missing `systemActorId` for `system` auth, late binding capture after `duplicatePageLayout`, and risk of bogus/double post-rollback attempts | Added `systemActorId: 'dashboard-duplication:duplicate'` for `system` auth in both calls, moved binding capture before `duplicatePageLayout`, and scoped `recordPostRollbackNativeCrmAuditAttempt` strictly to permission denials and `createDuplicatedDashboardOrRollback`'s `catch`. |
| m1 | minor | `recordPostRollbackNativeCrmAuditAttempt` and `getObjectMetadataId` must execute inside `executeInWorkspaceContext` in `DashboardSyncService` and `PageLayoutService.destroyAssociatedDashboards` | Moved `try/catch`, `getObjectMetadataId('dashboard')`, and `recordPostRollbackNativeCrmAuditAttempt` strictly inside `executeInWorkspaceContext`; clarified `resourceId` selection for `destroyAssociatedDashboards`. |
| m2 | minor | `DashboardSyncService` updates both live and soft-deleted dashboards matching `pageLayoutId` and is bounded by `QUERY_MAX_RECORDS` | Documented explicitly in `design.md` and `test-research.md`. |
| m3 | minor | Unlisted raw-SQL bootstrap/dev-seed writers (`prefillDashboards`, `DevSeederDataService`), `TrashCleanupService` `systemActorId` is `'trash_cleanup'`, and `buildRecordPosition` must stay outside `runInWorkspaceTransaction` | Listed bootstrap/dev-seed writers in `design.md` Out of scope, fixed `'trash_cleanup'` in `test-research.md`, and kept `buildRecordPosition` before `runInWorkspaceTransaction`. |
